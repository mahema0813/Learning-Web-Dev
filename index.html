Schezwan noodles

Schezwan Noodles is a popular Indo-Chinese dish made by stir-frying noodles with fresh vegetables and spicy Schezwan sauce. It is known for its bold, tangy, and spicy flavor, making it a favorite among people who enjoy hot and flavorful food. This dish is quick to prepareand is commonly served as a snack or main course.

Ingredients

    200 g noodles
    2 tablespoons Schezwan sauce
    1 tablespoon oil
    1 teaspoon garlic (finely chopped)
    1 small onion (sliced)
    1 small carrot (julienned)
    1/2 cup cabbage (shredded)
    1/2 capsicum (sliced)
    1 teaspoon soy sauce
    Salt to taste
    Spring onions for garnish

Recipe

    Boil the noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and rinse with cold water.
    Heat oil in a pan or wok and sauté the chopped garlic until fragrant.
    Add the onion, carrot, cabbage, and capsicum. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
    Add the Schezwan sauce and soy sauce, and mix well.
    Add the cooked noodles and toss everything together until the noodles are evenly coated with the sauce.
    Season with salt if needed and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
    Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve hot.
